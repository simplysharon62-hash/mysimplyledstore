Why runners need active visibility

Reflective gear helps when headlights shine directly on it, but clip-on LED lights create their own visibility. That makes them useful on side streets, trail crossings, parking lots, and early morning routes where drivers are distracted or tired.

A BLITZU LED clip can attach to a waistband, jacket, running belt, backpack, or shoes in seconds. The goal is simple: give drivers more distance and more time to recognize that a person is ahead.

Best places to clip a running safety light

  • Waistband or running belt for a moving light near the center of your body.
  • Jacket zipper or chest strap for front-facing visibility.
  • Backpack or hydration pack for rear visibility.
  • Shoes or ankle area for extra motion that catches attention.

Make your next run easier to see

Clip one on before you leave the house. It is a small habit with a big visibility benefit.
