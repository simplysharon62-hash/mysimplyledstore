Dog Walking Safety at Night: Visibility Tips

Your dog is lower, faster, and easier to miss in the dark. Add visible points for both of you.

Use two points of light

One light on your dog and one light on you gives drivers a clearer picture. A collar light marks the pet, while a jacket or leash light marks the walker.

Keep the leash visible

Dark leashes can disappear across sidewalks and driveways. A light near the leash handle or harness makes the walking pair easier to understand from a distance.

Choose safer routes

  • Use sidewalks and lighted streets when possible.
  • Avoid blind curves and dark shoulders.
  • Cross at intersections instead of between parked cars.
  • Turn on flashing mode near traffic.

Make evening walks easier to see

Clip a BLITZU LED light to a collar, leash, harness, jacket, or walking bag.
