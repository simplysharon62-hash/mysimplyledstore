What aged garlic extract is

Aged garlic extract is made by soaking fresh garlic for an extended period, often many months. The aging process mellows the harsh compounds in raw garlic, which is why aged garlic supplements are odorless and easier on the stomach. People often choose them because they're easier to take consistently than raw garlic.

What research says

Garlic has been studied for heart health for decades. Some small clinical studies of aged garlic extract have reported modest reductions in blood pressure in people with high blood pressure. The research is limited, results vary between studies, and a supplement is not a replacement for diagnosis, prescribed treatment or regular monitoring.

Before you try it

  • Keep taking prescribed medicine. Never stop or change blood pressure medication based on a supplement or someone else's experience.
  • Ask about interactions. Garlic supplements may increase bleeding risk, especially with blood thinners, and should be reviewed before surgery. Your pharmacist can check your medicine list.
  • Track your readings. A home blood pressure monitor and a simple log help you and your doctor see what's actually changing.
  • Look at the whole routine. Blood pressure responds to many things: salt, activity, sleep, stress, weight and medication.
