Common questions

What's a good gift for a parent who walks in the evening?

A rechargeable LED safety light or light-up vest. It helps drivers see them sooner, and it's useful every day once the evenings get dark.

Are resistance bands good for people over 50?

Bands are a popular way to do strength exercises at home because the resistance can start light and go up gradually. Anyone starting a new exercise routine should check with their doctor first.

What if they're allergic to latex?

Many resistance bands are made from latex. Look for bands labeled latex-free, and check the listing before you buy.
