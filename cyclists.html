Clip-On LED Lights for Cyclists

Bike lights are essential. Clip-on LED lights add extra moving visibility to your helmet, backpack, jacket, or saddle bag.

Why add clip lights when your bike already has lights?

Front and rear bike lights mark the bicycle. A clip-on light can mark the rider. That extra point of motion can help drivers notice you when you are turning, crossing intersections, walking the bike, or riding near parked cars.

Best cyclist uses for clip-on LEDs

  • Helmet or backpack for higher visibility.
  • Saddle bag or jacket for rear visibility.
  • Frame bag or commuting bag for side visibility.
  • Emergency spare light when a primary bike light dies.

Add a backup visibility light

Keep one in your bike bag so every late ride has another layer of visibility.
