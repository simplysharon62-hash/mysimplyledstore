Night Walking Safety Tips: How to Stay Visible

Most evening walks are routine. The danger is that drivers may not see you soon enough on dark streets.

Choose lighted routes first

Use sidewalks, crosswalks, and streets with working lights whenever possible. Familiar routes can still be risky if a streetlight is out or cars enter from side streets.

Wear visible layers

Light-colored clothing and reflective details help, but they depend on a light source. A clip-on LED light gives you active visibility even before headlights hit reflective material.

Put the light where it moves

Clip a light to your jacket zipper, waistband, backpack strap, or leash handle. A moving light is easier for drivers to notice than a dark silhouette.

Make visibility a habit

  • Clip on the light before leaving the house.
  • Use flashing mode near traffic.
  • Carry a backup light during travel.
  • Keep your phone free for emergencies, not as your only light.
