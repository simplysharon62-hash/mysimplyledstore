Why pet visibility matters

Dogs move lower to the ground and can be hidden by parked cars, shadows, landscaping, and poor street lighting. A bright clip on the collar, harness, or leash gives drivers and cyclists a moving visual cue.

Where to place a dog walking light

  • Attach one light to the collar or harness.
  • Add a second light to the leash handle or your jacket for two points of visibility.
  • Use flashing mode near roads and steady mode in parks or shared paths.
  • Keep a spare in your car or walking bag.

Clip up before every walk

One small light can make your dog easier to spot from a distance.
