The simple 5AM checklist

  • A clip-on LED safety light on your front or side.
  • Reflective details on shoes, vest, jacket, or running belt.
  • A route with sidewalks or wide shoulders.
  • A charged phone and identification.
  • A habit of facing traffic when walking on roads without sidewalks.

Why LED clips are useful for runners

They are small, light, and quick to attach. The moving light helps drivers recognize that a person is ahead, especially near intersections, driveways, and dim street corners.

How to use one without changing your run

Keep the clip with your shoes, running belt, or keys. Attach it before starting your watch. Use flashing mode near roads and steady mode on quiet shared paths.

Add active visibility to your kit

One clip can travel between running jackets, belts, backpacks, and shoes.
