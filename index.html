Featured wellness blog

Sometimes a Bigger Stomach Isn’t What You Think

When your stomach feels or looks larger than usual, it is easy to assume the change is body fat. But digestion can also change how your abdomen looks and feels from one day to the next.

Constipation can cause bloating, abdominal fullness, gas, and discomfort. When stool remains in the digestive tract longer than usual, some people may also notice a temporary change on the scale. That is different from long-term body-fat gain.

Start with the basics

Fiber supports normal bowel function, but suddenly adding a large amount can sometimes increase gas or bloating. A gradual increase, along with enough fluids, is usually easier to tolerate.

Foods that naturally contain fiber include beans and lentils, oats and whole grains, berries and other fruits, vegetables, nuts, and seeds.

Healing thyself does not mean ignoring medicine

Natural wellness can support the foundations your body already depends on: nutrition, hydration, movement, sleep, stress management, and digestive regularity. Supplements can help fill gaps, but they should complement those foundations rather than replace appropriate medical care.

Persistent abdominal swelling, severe pain, vomiting, blood in the stool, or inability to pass stool or gas should be medically evaluated rather than treated by simply adding more fiber.

This article is for general education and is not medical advice. Individual responses to foods and supplements vary. Review product labels and speak with a qualified health professional when appropriate.

Aged Garlic & Blood Pressure: Godwin's Personal Experience

A personal wellness story, plus practical reminders about supplements and blood pressure.

Godwin's experience

Why aged garlic stayed in his routine

Godwin started using aged garlic as part of his daily wellness routine during a period when he was dealing with high blood pressure. Over time, his blood pressure readings improved significantly.

Important:

We cannot say aged garlic alone caused that improvement or that it cured high blood pressure. What we can say is that aged garlic became an important part of the routine Godwin continued while his readings improved.

What aged garlic is

Aged garlic supplements are made from garlic that has been processed and aged over time. People often choose them because they are easier to use consistently than raw garlic and are commonly included in heart-health wellness routines.

Godwin's approach

For Godwin, the supplement was not about trying to fix everything overnight. It became one part of a broader routine that included paying attention to daily habits and staying consistent.

What to keep in mind

  • Personal results can vary.
  • Blood pressure can change for many reasons, including medication, diet, activity, stress, sleep, and other health factors.
  • Supplements can interact with medicines, so it is important to review them with a healthcare professional or pharmacist.
  • Do not stop prescribed blood-pressure medicine based on a supplement or someone else's experience.
